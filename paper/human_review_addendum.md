## Additional author review of unresolved pairs

On November 13, 2026, the author completed an additional review of the 200 pairs that had remained unresolved after the original two-reviewer assessment. The submitted judgments use preservation of the main event and overall context as the criterion, allowing some factual differences. The author confirmed this interpretation after the difference from the earlier fact-preservation guidance was identified. Under this criterion, 163 pairs (81.5%) were accepted and 37 (18.5%) were rejected; all 200 were judged to retain the original news category.

These judgments are a separate, post-experiment author assessment. They are not adjudicated consensus between the original reviewers, an additional independent two-reviewer study, or evidence of strict semantic equivalence. The 200 pairs were selected because they were unresolved, so their acceptance rate cannot estimate the quality of the full 22,800-variant bank. The original independent judgments, 68.67% agreement, and nominal kappa of approximately 0.150 remain unchanged. We do not pool assessments made under different criteria into a revised bank-quality estimate.

All 200 submitted source and variant texts exactly match the preserved records. Excel had converted the review identifiers to numeric time values; identifiers were restored through unique exact source–variant matches, with a row-level restoration trace. No judgment or explanation was altered. The submitted workbook is retained byte-for-byte. This review changed no paraphrase, model checkpoint, PER value, or predictive result.
